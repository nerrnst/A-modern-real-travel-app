Travel guide

Plan smarter, travel deeper.

Useful notes on the best seasons, destination tips, packing essentials, and cultural etiquette for your African journey.

📅

Best time to visit

Dry seasons are ideal for safaris, while coastal destinations shine in calmer, sunny months.

🎒

What to pack

Light layers, sun protection, comfortable walking shoes, and a camera for unforgettable wildlife moments.

🧭

Travel tips

Respect local customs, carry cash where useful, and leave space for unplanned discoveries.
